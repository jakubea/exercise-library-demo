Romanian Deadlift

The Romanian Deadlift is a intermediate compound exercise for the legs, with help from the glutes, back. It uses barbell.

How to do it

  1. Hold the bar at the hips with a soft knee bend
  2. Push the hips back and slide the bar down the thighs
  3. Squeeze the glutes to stand tall

Common mistakes

  • Bending the knees too much so it becomes a squat
  • Rounding the back
