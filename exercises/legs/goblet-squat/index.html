Goblet Squat

The Goblet Squat is a beginner compound exercise for the legs, with help from the glutes, core. It uses kettlebell.

How to do it

  1. Hold a kettlebell at the chest with the elbows pointing down
  2. Squat down between the knees keeping the chest tall
  3. Stand up by pushing through the floor

Common mistakes

  • Heels lifting off the floor
  • Leaning forward and dropping the weight
