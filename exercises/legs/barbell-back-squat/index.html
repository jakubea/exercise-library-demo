Barbell Back Squat

The Barbell Back Squat is a intermediate compound exercise for the legs, with help from the glutes, core. It uses barbell.

How to do it

  1. Rest the bar on the upper back and set feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Sit down and back until thighs are at least parallel
  3. Drive through the whole foot to stand

Common mistakes

  • Knees caving inward
  • Rounding the lower back at the bottom
