Walking Lunge

The Walking Lunge is a beginner compound exercise for the legs, with help from the glutes. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Hold dumbbells at your sides and step forward into a long stride
  2. Lower the back knee toward the floor
  3. Push through the front foot into the next step

Common mistakes

  • Taking steps that are too short
  • Letting the front knee collapse inward
