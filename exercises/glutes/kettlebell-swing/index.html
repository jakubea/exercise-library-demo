Kettlebell Swing

The Kettlebell Swing is a intermediate compound exercise for the glutes, with help from the legs, back. It uses kettlebell.

How to do it

  1. Hinge at the hips and hold the kettlebell with both hands
  2. Snap the hips forward to swing the bell to chest height
  3. Let it fall back and hinge again

Common mistakes

  • Squatting instead of hinging
  • Lifting with the arms
