Hip Thrust

The Hip Thrust is a intermediate compound exercise for the glutes, with help from the legs. It uses barbell.

How to do it

  1. Sit with the upper back against a bench and the bar over the hips
  2. Drive the hips up until the body forms a straight line
  3. Lower slowly and repeat

Common mistakes

  • Overarching the lower back at the top
  • Pushing through the toes instead of the heels
