Glute Bridge

The Glute Bridge is a beginner compound exercise for the glutes, with help from the legs. It needs no equipment.

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat
  2. Squeeze the glutes and lift the hips
  3. Lower under control

Common mistakes

  • Pushing the lower back up instead of the hips
  • Letting the knees fall apart
