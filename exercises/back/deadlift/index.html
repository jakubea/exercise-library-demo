Deadlift

The Deadlift is a advanced compound exercise for the back, with help from the glutes, hamstrings. It uses barbell.

How to do it

  1. Stand with the bar over mid-foot and grip just outside the knees
  2. Brace the core and push the floor away while keeping the bar close
  3. Lock out the hips at the top then lower the bar along the legs

Common mistakes

  • Rounding the back at the start
  • Jerking the bar off the floor
