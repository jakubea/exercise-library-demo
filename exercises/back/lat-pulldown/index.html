Lat Pulldown

The Lat Pulldown is a beginner compound exercise for the back, with help from the biceps. It uses machine.

How to do it

  1. Sit with thighs secured under the pads and grip the bar wide
  2. Pull the bar to the upper chest while keeping the torso tall
  3. Return slowly until the arms are straight

Common mistakes

  • Leaning far back to swing the weight
  • Pulling the bar behind the neck
