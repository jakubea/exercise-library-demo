Dead Bug

The Dead Bug is a beginner isolation exercise for the core. It needs no equipment.

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back with arms up and knees bent at 90 degrees
  2. Extend the opposite arm and leg while pressing the lower back into the floor
  3. Return and switch sides

Common mistakes

  • Letting the lower back arch
  • Moving too fast
