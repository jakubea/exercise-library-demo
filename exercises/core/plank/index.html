Plank

The Plank is a beginner isolation exercise for the core, with help from the shoulders. It needs no equipment.

How to do it

  1. Rest on forearms and toes with elbows under the shoulders
  2. Brace the abs and keep the body in one straight line
  3. Hold for the planned time

Common mistakes

  • Letting the hips sag or pike
  • Holding the breath
