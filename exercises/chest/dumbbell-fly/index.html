Dumbbell Fly

The Dumbbell Fly is a intermediate isolation exercise for the chest, with help from the shoulders. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Lie on a flat bench with dumbbells above the chest and palms facing in
  2. Open the arms in a wide arc with a slight elbow bend
  3. Squeeze the chest to bring the weights back together

Common mistakes

  • Going too heavy and turning it into a press
  • Lowering the arms far below shoulder level
