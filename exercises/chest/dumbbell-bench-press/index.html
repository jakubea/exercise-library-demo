Dumbbell Bench Press

The Dumbbell Bench Press is a beginner compound exercise for the chest, with help from the triceps, shoulders. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Sit on the bench with dumbbells on your thighs and lie back
  2. Lower the weights to chest level with wrists stacked over elbows
  3. Press up and bring the dumbbells together above the chest

Common mistakes

  • Letting the dumbbells drift too far apart
  • Arching the lower back off the bench
