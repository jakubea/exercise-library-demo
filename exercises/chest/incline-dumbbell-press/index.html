Incline Dumbbell Press

The Incline Dumbbell Press is a intermediate compound exercise for the chest, with help from the shoulders, triceps. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Set the bench to 30 degrees and lie back with dumbbells at shoulder height
  2. Press up until the arms are straight
  3. Lower slowly to the upper chest

Common mistakes

  • Setting the bench too steep so the shoulders take over
  • Using weight that shortens the range of motion
