Pike Push-Up

The Pike Push-Up is a intermediate compound exercise for the shoulders, with help from the triceps. It needs no equipment.

How to do it

  1. Start in a downward-dog position with hips high
  2. Bend the elbows to lower the head toward the floor
  3. Press back up to the start

Common mistakes

  • Keeping the hips low like a regular push-up
  • Flaring the elbows wide
