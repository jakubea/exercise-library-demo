Dumbbell Lateral Raise

The Dumbbell Lateral Raise is a beginner isolation exercise for the shoulders. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Stand with light dumbbells at your sides and a slight elbow bend
  2. Raise the arms out to shoulder height
  3. Lower slowly without swinging

Common mistakes

  • Using heavy weights and swinging the body
  • Lifting the hands above the elbows' line
