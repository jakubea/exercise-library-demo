Overhead Press

The Overhead Press is a intermediate compound exercise for the shoulders, with help from the triceps, core. It uses barbell.

How to do it

  1. Hold the bar at collarbone height with the elbows slightly forward
  2. Squeeze the glutes and press the bar straight overhead
  3. Lower back to the collarbone

Common mistakes

  • Leaning back excessively
  • Pressing the bar out in front instead of overhead
