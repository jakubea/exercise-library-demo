Dumbbell Hammer Curl

The Dumbbell Hammer Curl is a beginner isolation exercise for the biceps, with help from the forearms. It uses dumbbell.

How to do it

  1. Hold dumbbells at your sides with palms facing each other
  2. Curl the weights up without rotating the wrists
  3. Lower under control

Common mistakes

  • Using momentum from the hips
  • Shrugging the shoulders
