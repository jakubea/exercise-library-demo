Cable Curl

The Cable Curl is a beginner isolation exercise for the biceps, with help from the forearms. It uses cable.

How to do it

  1. Face a low pulley and hold the bar with an underhand grip
  2. Curl the bar toward the shoulders keeping the elbows pinned
  3. Lower until the arms are straight

Common mistakes

  • Leaning back to lift the weight
  • Releasing tension at the bottom
