Triceps Pushdown

The Triceps Pushdown is a beginner isolation exercise for the triceps. It uses cable.

How to do it

  1. Stand at a high pulley with the elbows tucked at your sides
  2. Push the handle down until the arms are straight
  3. Return until the forearms are just past parallel

Common mistakes

  • Letting the elbows flare out
  • Leaning over the weight
