Bench Dip

The Bench Dip is a beginner compound exercise for the triceps, with help from the chest, shoulders. It needs no equipment.

How to do it

  1. Sit on a bench edge with hands beside the hips and legs extended
  2. Slide off and lower the body by bending the elbows
  3. Press back up to straight arms

Common mistakes

  • Dropping too deep and stressing the shoulders
  • Drifting far away from the bench
